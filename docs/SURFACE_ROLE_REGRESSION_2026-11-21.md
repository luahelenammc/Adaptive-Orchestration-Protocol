## Review limits and retained constraints

These are reasoned scenario evaluations against the text, not execution traces or empirical guarantees. CEF-12–14 require real source, provider and native-skill observations; they remain `NOT_TESTED`. Build reproducibility, package contents, hashes, cross-repository projections and deployment are separate tests. This review did not independently compare every unchanged word to 6.4 or prove absence of all contradictions.

The candidate retains the existing routing dimensions, `ROUTE_AND_EXECUTE`, `ROUTE_ONLY` and `AUDIT_ROUTE`, acceptance states, scope and permission boundaries, control-root continuity, Behavioral Fit, Decision Commitment, Delegation-First and human authority. Placement is a sub-gate, not a new universal cross-surface sequence. No universal quota, surface superiority or model ranking follows from the community examples. A separate reviewer is conditional on availability and cost, and independence cannot be established by repeating the same self-report.
